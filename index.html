Play with the stars ✨

  • ✋Drag the middle of a star to move it. Let go while moving to throw it!
  • 🔄Grab a star's points and move around it in a circle to turn it. It keeps spinning that way.
  • ✏️Press and drag on an empty spot to draw your own star.
  • 👆Tap an empty spot for a brand new picture.
  • 🥞Double-click a star to flip which way it spins.
